Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Clip Drafts only stores the access token that the account owner grants when connecting their own TikTok account, plus the account's open ID. These are used solely to upload the owner's own videos as drafts.

No other personal data is collected. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), after which the stored token is deleted.
